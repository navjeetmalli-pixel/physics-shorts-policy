Physics Shorts Uploader is a personal, single-user application that publishes original, computer-generated physics simulation videos (bouncing balls, rotating rings, synthesized music) to the owner's own YouTube channel using YouTube API Services. It is not distributed to, or usable by, anyone other than the channel owner.

What it does

  • Uploads finished simulation videos to the owner's channel (videos.insert)
  • Sets each video's title, description, tags and thumbnail
  • Creates a playlist and adds each video to them
  • Reads back the owner's own channel, video and playlist details to confirm success

What it does not do

  • It never accesses any other user's YouTube data
  • It stores no viewer or subscriber data
  • It does not embed, play or modify the YouTube player
